FAQ

Common questions before getting started.

Do you only work with health and fitness businesses?

No. I work broadly with SMEs, while maintaining a particular interest in health, fitness, nutrition, and sport-related work.

Can you work with messy spreadsheets or incomplete data?

Yes. Cleaning, restructuring, and improving imperfect data is often a core part of the project.

Do you build AI solutions for every project?

No. AI is used where it genuinely adds value. In many cases, clearer reporting, better processes, or simpler automation is the smarter option.

Can you train our team as part of the project?

Yes. Training can be delivered as part of an implementation so your team understands how to use the tools, where they fit, and what good practice looks like in your business.
